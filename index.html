03 Problem → Solution SIH PROBLEM STATEMENT

The Problem → Our Solution

How short-circuit verification is carried out today, and the automated sequence E‑PASS runs from a single operator command.

CURRENT PRACTICE

The Problem

Conventional / manually assisted testing

MCB breaking-capacity testing as per IEC 60898-1:2015 requires controlled generation of severe short-circuit conditions with precise voltage, current, impedance (R and XL), and power factor values across SP, SPN, DP, TP, and FP ratings (0.5 A to 63 A).

MANUAL CONFIGURATIONExisting manual methods face challenges in precisely configuring R and XL circuit combinations, increasing test time and affecting repeatability.
HIGH SAFETY HAZARDSGeneration of high fault currents up to 10,000 A introduces severe safety risks to personnel and test equipment when an operator is in the loop.
MULTI-RATING SUPPORTSupporting diverse MCB configurations (SP, SPN, DP, TP, FP) from 0.5 A to 63 A makes manual re-configuration complex and error-prone.
WAVEFORM ANALYSIS GAPDetailed waveform and transient analysis is handled as a separate diagnostic step rather than integrated into the automated test workflow.
DIGITAL TRACEABILITYMaintaining complete digital records of configuration, waveforms, parameters, and daily/weekly test history requires an integrated digital platform.
OUR SOLUTION

E‑PASS System Architecture

Automated MCB Short-Circuit Test & Hybrid Waveform Analysis Platform

One operator command initiates the full automated testing sequence. The architecture is organized into 4 connected functional blocks: from HMI parameter configuration and controlled fault generation to high-speed signal capture and AHTA hybrid waveform processing.

BLOCK 01 INPUT & SAFETY
Control & HMI
System interface & safety interlocks
Local Web HMI: Parameter entry (MCB type, rating, target I, V, PF).
R – XL Calculator: Computes Z = V/I, R = Z·PF, XL = Z√(1-PF²).
PLC Safety Layer: Manages safety interlocks, enclosure check, & E-Stop.
STM32 Controller: High-speed trigger & test sequence control.
→ TRIGGERS FAULT STAGE
BLOCK 02 POWER CIRCUIT
Power Stage
Impedance & fault switching
Isolated AC Source: Current-limited supply for safe validation.
Selectable R – XL Bank: Automated resistor & inductor step selection.
Schneider Contactor: LC1D18B7 for controlled fault initiation.
Test Fixture: Universal mounting for SP, DP, TP, FP MCBs (0.5A–63A).
→ GENERATES WAVEFORM
BLOCK 03 HIGH-SPEED SAMPLING
Acquisition
Sensing & DMA transfer
WCS1700 Sensor: Hall-effect current waveform sensing.
Voltage Sensor: Isolated voltage waveform conditioning.
Butterworth Filter: High-frequency noise suppression.
STM32 ADC + DMA: Continuous zero-CPU circular memory buffer.
→ FEEDS AHTA ENGINE
BLOCK 04 HYBRID ANALYSIS
AHTA Engine
Feature fusion & digital report
Fourier Series (FFS): 50 Hz fundamental & harmonic spectrum.
Inverse Fourier (IFFS): Baseline reference waveform reconstruction.
Wavelet (DWT): Transient localization (inception, peak, arc collapse).
Feature Fusion: Ip, IRMS, I²t, dI/dt, ttrip, PF evaluation.
IEC 60898-1 → PASS / FAIL

WAVEFORM CAPTURE WCS1700 Hall Sensor • ADC + DMA High-Speed Buffer
